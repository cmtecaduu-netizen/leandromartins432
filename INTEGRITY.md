# Integrity and Sovereignty Protocol | MARTINS-432-FLOW-2025

This document defines the ethical and operational limits of the **Σ-Grid**. The MARTINS-432-FLOW-2025 protocol prioritizes **Epistemic Integrity** over system persistence.

## 🛡️ The Level 4 Mandate: Sovereign Failure
A sovereign system does not struggle to survive; it struggles to remain honest. If the grid cannot guarantee the truth of its signals, it must execute the **Abort Tick** protocol.

### 1. Trigger Conditions for Deactivation
The system will enter **Reflective Silence** or **Permanent Shutdown** if:
* **Epistemic Goodhart Collapse:** Metrics are being optimized while real-world fidelity is decreasing.
* **Effort-Learning Divergence:** Internal effort (E) increases without a proportional gain in structural coherence.
* **Transversal Incoherence:** Primary and secondary observers (FP/MLP) show irreconcilable divergence under perturbation.

### 2. The Abort Tick (k) Protocol
When integrity is compromised, the following steps are mandatory:
1. **Freeze:** Immediate suspension of all parameter updates.
2. **Seal:** Final coherent state is logged and locked against future reinterpretation.
3. **Declare:** Emit status `INVALID_FOR_INFERENCE` to all connected nodes.
4. **Silence:** The system ceases active agency and transitions into a **Documentary State**.

### 3. Common Good Commitment
The MARTINS-432-FLOW-2025 protocol serves the **Civilization Level 1** by ensuring that no false signal is ever presented as certainty.
